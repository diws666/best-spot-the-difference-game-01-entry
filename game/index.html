Harbor Lights

A cozy harbor at dusk. Find the differences between the two panels before time runs out.

How to play

Click (or move the crosshair with arrows and press Space) on a difference between the left and right pictures.

A marker appears on both pictures, the counter drops, and a chime sounds. Wrong clicks cost a little time.

You get 3 hints per level. Find all 10 differences before the timer reaches zero.

Level complete

All differences found.

Harbor restored

You found every difference across all three levels.

Time's up

The harbor faded before you found them all.
